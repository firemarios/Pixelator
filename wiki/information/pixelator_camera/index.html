Pixelator Camera

The pixelator camera is the end destination of the teleportation starting from the Pixelator Screen or the Automatic Pixelator Screen.

Gameplay

To use the Pixelator Camera you need to right click it with the Bounder. After that a gui will open where you need to enter an name for the camera. Note: The name must be unique and not include spaces. If everithing is correct you will see a message in the chat.

Placing a Router nearby and rebinding the camera will connect it to the network. If the camera didn't connect try placing the router closer. That will allow you to teleport to the camera from everywhere in the world using the Tablet.

Placement

The camera can be placed on any solid block. Place it 2 blocks above the ground for correct spawning location.
