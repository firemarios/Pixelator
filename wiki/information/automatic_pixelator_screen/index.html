Automatic Pixelator Screen

The automatic pixelator screen is as the name says an Automatic Pixelator Screen.

Gameplay

To use the Automatic Pixelator Screen you need to right click and in the gui select a camera or search for it. You cannot teleport to private cameras. Connect redstone to it and activate it. The Automatic Pixelator Screen will teleport every player in a 10 block radius to the camera.

Placement

Place the Automatic Pixelator Screen on the ground and wait for it to activate. To trigger it connect it with redstone.
